<p><i>YumeList</i> is an unofficial MyAnimeList client for Android. Track what you watch and read, see what's airing today, browse the season and explore the rankings, all in a clean Material 3 Expressive interface with full OLED black support.</p><p><br><b>Track</b></p><ul><li>Manage your anime and manga lists, synced with MyAnimeList</li><li>One-tap +1 progress buttons with progress bars</li><li>"Airing in X days" countdowns</li><li>Sort, filter and choose your list style</li><li>Edit status, score, progress and dates in one sheet</li></ul><p><b>Discover</b></p><ul><li>Today: what's airing now, with broadcast times</li><li>Seasonal chart with filters and a season switcher</li><li>Weekly airing calendar</li><li>Top anime and manga rankings</li><li>Search with history</li></ul><p><b>Details</b></p><ul><li>Score, rank, popularity and member stats</li><li>Next broadcast time, genres, synopsis, characters and related titles</li><li>Opening and ending themes</li><li>Status distribution and manga statistics</li></ul><p><b>Make it yours</b></p><ul><li>Light, dark or system theme, plus pure black mode</li><li>Smooth shared-element transitions</li><li>Custom start tab and tablet layout</li><li>Airing notifications and a home-screen widget</li></ul><p>YumeList is a fork of MoeList by axiel7 and is licensed under GPL-3.0.</p>
