Built for people who keep their MyAnimeList up to date and want it to feel good on their phone.

What's airing today

A Today carousel on Home shows every episode dropping today, with broadcast times and live "Airing in 3h" countdowns. Your list shows exactly how many days until the next episode.

One-tap +1

Finished an episode or chapter? Tap +1 and your MyAnimeList is updated instantly.

Season & calendar

Browse any season with filters, or check the weekly schedule day by day.

Top rankings

The best anime and manga by score, popularity and favorites, plus upcoming hits.

Notifications & widget

Get notified when a show airs, and keep today's schedule on your home screen.

Material 3 Expressive

Bouncy motion, shape-morphing elements and smooth shared-element transitions. Choose light, dark or system, plus a pure black mode for OLED screens.

Deep details

Score, rank, popularity, next broadcast, characters, related titles, opening and ending themes, and status stats, all on one screen.

FAQ

Questions?

Is YumeList really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no trackers. The full source code is on GitHub.

Do I need a MyAnimeList account?

You can browse rankings, seasons and the calendar without one. To track your own list, sign in with MyAnimeList; YumeList uses MAL's official login, so your password is never shared with the app.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. For now, download the APK from GitHub Releases. Your phone may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Which APK should I download?

For most phones, pick arm64-v8a. If you're not sure, the universal APK works on every device; it's just a little bigger.

How is it related to MoeList?

YumeList is a fork of MoeList by axiel7. It has since become its own app, with a full Material 3 Expressive redesign, a rewritten network layer and modernized internals. All credit for the original foundation goes to axiel7.
