Questions?

Is YumeList really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no trackers. The full source code is on GitHub.

Do I need a MyAnimeList account?

No. Tap "Continue as guest" to browse Home, seasons, rankings, the calendar and details. To track your own list, sign in with MyAnimeList. YumeList uses MAL's official login page, so the app never sees your password.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Download the APK from GitHub Releases, or get automatic updates through Orion Store. Your phone may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Which APK should I download?

For most phones, pick arm64-v8a. If you're not sure, the universal APK works on every device; it's just a little bigger.

Can I see NSFW titles?

They're hidden by default. Turn on "Show NSFW content" while setting up the app, or later in Settings, Content.

How is it related to MoeList?

YumeList is a fork of MoeList by axiel7. It has since become its own app, with a full Material 3 Expressive redesign, a rewritten network layer and modernized internals. All credit for the original foundation goes to axiel7.
